About Us

Our Project

We are passionate about building a better, eco-friendly future for everyone.

The goal of our project is to game-ify environmentalism. We wanted to give the users an opportunity to have fun and connect with their community and encourage them to be mindful of the environmental impacts of their actions. As we all know, global warming is getting worse by the second and it is up to our generation to reverse the damage that has been done. But, this change cannot just come from one person, we have to take action as a community to make a change. Our website is a solution to this problem. It bridges individuals with their communities to bridge our current, hopeless looking present, with a bright ecological future.
